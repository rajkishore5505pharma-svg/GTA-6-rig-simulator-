How the GTA 6 Rig & FPS Simulator handles affiliate links, cookies, and analytics, and what our FPS numbers do and don't mean. Last updated: November 22, 2026.

1. Amazon Associates & Partner Link Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

This site takes part in the Amazon Associates program for Amazon.in (India) and Amazon.com (US), an affiliate advertising program that lets sites earn fees by linking to Amazon. The "Check Latest Price on Amazon" buttons next to each component are affiliate links.

Where the links go

When you click one, you are sent to Amazon.in if the simulator is set to INR, or to Amazon.com if it is set to USD. Each link carries our affiliate tracking tag so Amazon can credit the visit to this site. Where we don't have a specific product page, the button opens an Amazon search for that part's name.

What it costs you

Nothing. If you buy a qualifying product after clicking through, we may earn a small commission, and the price you pay on Amazon is the same. These links are marked nofollow sponsored so search engines know they are paid referrals.

Prices

Prices on this site are estimated guide prices for planning. They are not live Amazon prices, and the buttons deliberately show no price. The price and availability shown on Amazon when you buy are the ones that apply.

Amazon and the Amazon logo are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates. This site is not operated by or endorsed by Amazon.

Links to Boosteroid (cloud gaming), Eneba (game keys, gift cards and wallet codes), and Bixberry (a rewards app that pays users in gift cards) are also affiliate or referral links, and they are marked nofollow sponsored as well. If you sign up or buy through them, we may earn a commission or referral reward at no extra cost to you. Their prices, bonuses, availability, and terms are set by those companies and can change, so check them on their own sites.

2. Cookies, Third-Party Services & Analytics

Cookies set by Amazon

After you click through to Amazon, Amazon may set or read standard browser cookies so it can credit the referral to our affiliate account and run your shopping session. Amazon controls those cookies under the privacy notice and cookie notice on Amazon.in or Amazon.com.

Cookies from other partners

Boosteroid, Eneba, and Bixberry may also set cookies after you click through, so they can credit the referral. We don't control those cookies, and each company's own privacy policy explains how it uses them.

This site's own cookies

The simulator has no accounts, forms, or sign-ups, and it does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies. Your selections live in the page address (see section 4), not in a profile about you.

Analytics

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics service to count aggregate visits, such as pages viewed, referring site, browser or device type, and approximate country. Such a service should not use cookies to follow you across other sites, and we do not use analytics to identify individual visitors or collect names or email addresses.

Third-party services this page loads

As with any website, each of these can see your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their files:

  • Tailwind CSS (cdn.tailwindcss.com) and Google Fonts, which style the page.
  • three.js and html2canvas from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. three.js draws the 3D rig with WebGL on your own device, and html2canvas builds the downloadable PNG card in your browser.
  • Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org), which supplies the reference part photos. When a part is shown, your browser searches Commons for a free-licensed photo using the part's name, then loads the image. Wikimedia can see that search text along with your IP address. Photos are credited to their authors where Commons provides it, and may show a similar model.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this site and may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • Use a content blocker or your browser's privacy features to limit third-party requests.
  • Blocking partner cookies may stop a purchase or sign-up from being credited to us. The simulator works the same either way, though blocked photos or 3D libraries will not appear.

3. FPS Projection Disclaimer

FPS figures are independent estimates, not official Rockstar Games benchmarks.

The GTA 6 PC FPS projections, bottleneck warnings, and VRAM checks on this site are independent community and hardware estimates. They come from a simplified model built on publicly available RAGE engine benchmarks and the current specifications of GPUs and CPUs, including VRAM, relative performance, and core strength.

They are not official system requirements and are not measurements from the finished PC game. Real performance will vary with drivers, game patches, RAM speed, cooling, background software, and your settings.

Build presets and part picks are starting points. Before you buy, confirm compatibility yourself, including CPU socket, RAM type, power supply capacity, and case clearance.

This site is an independent fan and hardware-planning project. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names are trademarks of their respective owners.

4. Shareable Links, Clipboard & Image Export

When you change a setting, your choices (CPU, GPU, resolution, upscaling, region, graphics preset, budget, and any ready-made build you loaded) are written into the page address as query parameters so you can copy and share a link. The simulator does not send those choices to any server of ours, and the link contains no personal information. Anyone you give the link to, or post where others can see it, can see those choices.

The share link and the Reddit / Discord spec sheet are copied to your clipboard only when you tap the matching button. The PNG build card is generated in your browser and saved to your device. This site does not upload the clipboard text or the image, and the 3D viewer does not upload anything either.
